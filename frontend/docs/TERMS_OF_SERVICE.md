## What Velar is (and isn't)

Velar reads Google Pay transaction statement PDFs that you upload and
turns them into a spending overview, categorization, recurring-payment
detection, and a searchable transaction history.

Velar is **not**:
- A bank, payment processor, or financial institution
- Able to move money, initiate payments, or access your bank/UPI account
  directly - it only reads a PDF file you choose to give it
- A source of financial, tax, or investment advice. Categorization and
  "signals" are automated best-effort output, not professional advice -
  verify anything that matters against your actual bank/UPI records
  before acting on it.

## Accuracy of statement processing

Statement parsing and categorization are automated and can be wrong -
misread amounts, mis-parsed dates, misclassified merchants, or missed
transactions are all possible, especially on statement formats that
differ from what the parser has been built and tested against. The "why
this category" explanations shown in the app describe the automated
reasoning that produced a result; they are not a guarantee of accuracy.
Cross-check anything you rely on financially against your actual Google
Pay / bank records.
