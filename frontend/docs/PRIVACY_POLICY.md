**Status: draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Velar is not currently
published on any app store. This document accurately describes what the
app and its backend actually do as of the date below - it is written to be
factual, not aspirational - but it has not received formal legal review

## What Velar is

Velar is a personal finance app. You upload a Google Pay transaction
statement (a PDF you export from Google Pay yourself), and Velar parses it
to show you a spending overview, category breakdowns, recurring-payment
detection, and a searchable transaction history. Velar is not a bank, is
not a payment processor, and cannot move money - it only reads statements
you choose to give it.

## What data we collect

**Account data**
- Email address (used to sign in; not editable after registration)
- Password - never stored in plain text. It's hashed with Argon2id
  (the current OWASP-recommended algorithm) before it touches the
  database; the plain-text password is discarded immediately after
  hashing and is not logged.
- Display name, if you choose to set one (optional, editable in Profile)

**Statement data**
- The Google Pay statement PDF you upload
- Every transaction line parsed out of it: merchant/counterparty name,
  amount, a best-effort spending category, timestamp, transaction type
  (debit/credit), status, the UPI reference number, the paying bank's
  name, and the last 4 digits of the account number (never the full
  account number)

**Data you actively provide**
- Category corrections ("Wrong category" on any transaction) - used to
  improve future categorization for your account and, in aggregate,
  the underlying merchant-recognition rules
- Your display name, if set

**What we do not collect**
- No location data
- No device contacts, camera, or microphone access
- No advertising identifiers
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are integrated into the app -
  nothing about your usage is sent to a third-party analytics vendor,
  because there isn't one in this app

## How your data is processed

Statement parsing, merchant categorization, and the natural-language
explanations you see in the app ("Why this category") run on
infrastructure Velar's developer operates directly:

- The database (MongoDB) and vector search index (Milvus) that store your
  data are self-hosted, not a third-party managed database service.
- Categorization and explanation text are generated by a locally-run
  language model (Ollama), not a third-party AI API. Your transaction
  data is not sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any other external AI
  provider.
- No third-party processor sees your statement data as part of normal
  operation.

## How your data is used

- To show you the overview, signals, activity, and analytics screens in
  the app
- To improve categorization accuracy for your own future statements,
  based on corrections you submit
- To send you optional local notifications (statement finished
  processing, unusual spend detected) - these are generated and shown
  entirely by the app on your device; no push-notification service
  receives your data to deliver them

Your data is never sold, and it is not shared with third parties for
their own marketing or analytics purposes. This is a design commitment,
not just a policy statement - there is currently no code path in the app
or backend that shares your data with an external party.

## Data retention and deletion

- **Deleting a single statement:** Manage Periods (Profile → Manage
  periods) lets you delete any statement at any time. This permanently
  removes that statement's stored PDF, all of its parsed transactions,
  and its processing history. This action cannot be undone.
- **Deleting your whole account:** self-service account deletion isn't
  built into the app yet. Email the contact address above to request full
  account and data deletion, and we'll confirm once it's done.
- **Signing out:** clears your session from that device only; it does not
  delete any server-side data.
- Uploaded PDFs are stored so the statement can be re-viewed and its
  transactions re-listed without re-uploading; they are removed when you
  delete that statement (or your account).

## Security

- Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, never stored or logged in plain
  text.
- Sessions use short-lived JWT access tokens (15 minutes) plus a rotating
  refresh token; refresh tokens are single-use and revoked on reuse
  (a signal we treat as possible token theft).
- All traffic to the backend is required to be HTTPS except for local
  development against `localhost`.
- On your device, session tokens are stored using platform-level secure
  storage (Android's encrypted shared preferences / iOS Keychain), not
  plain app storage.

No system is perfectly secure, and self-hosted infrastructure run by an
individual developer does not carry the same operational guarantees as a
large regulated financial institution. Use your judgment about what
statements you upload.

## Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can at any time:
- See exactly what's stored about a statement (it's in the app - nothing
  is hidden from you)
- Delete any individual statement yourself, immediately, from Manage
  Periods
- Request full account deletion by emailing the contact address above
- Export your transaction data as CSV (Profile → Export my data)

## Children's privacy

Velar is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect
data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account,
contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects how your data is
handled, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where
practical, notify you in-app.
